# Irad Web Mirror

Copies of Iradi websites, kept by volunteer editors of [Irad Wiki](https://irad.wiki/). We call it
[Project Kozgu](https://irad.wiki/wiki/Project_Kozgu), from *közgü*, "mirror".

From late 2023 to the summer of 2026 most sites hosted in Irad could not be opened from abroad. Attelkom's gateways
showed foreign visitors a "technical work" page, and foreign search engines all but dropped the country. Those of us
editing the wiki from outside still needed the sources, so we began copying the public pages of government offices,
newspapers and companies here, where anyone can read them.

The gateways opened again in 2026. We kept going: several of the sites now publish through these copies directly,
so they stay reachable the next time the national segment is switched on.

## What we mirror

Published, public pages only: no forums, no member areas, nothing behind a login. Each repository holds one site and
is synced from it. The site's own address works as before; the copy sits behind it.
